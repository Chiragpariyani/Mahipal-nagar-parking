How it works

  1. Blocked? Type the number plate (or the last 4 digits) above.
  2. Tap Call or WhatsApp to reach the owner. The WhatsApp message is already written for you.
  3. Not listed? Ask the owner to register, it takes one minute.

Parking etiquette

  • Register every vehicle you park in the common plot.
  • Do not block another vehicle. If you must, keep your phone reachable.
  • Leave your tenement number visible on the dashboard.
  • Park inside the marked area and keep the lane clear for emergency vehicles.
  • Update your details if you sell your vehicle or change your number.
  • Be polite. A friendly call solves it faster than an angry one.
